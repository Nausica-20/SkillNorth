      <span class="eyebrow">THE PATH</span>

      <h2>Build your data skills step by step.</h2>

      <p>
        A strong analyst combines technical ability with curiosity,
        problem-solving and clear communication.
      </p>
    </div>

    <div class="steps-grid">

      <div class="step">
        <span class="step-number">01</span>
        <h3>Data Fundamentals</h3>
        <p>
          Understand datasets, variables, tables, metrics and basic
          analytical thinking.
        </p>
      </div>

      <div class="step">
        <span class="step-number">02</span>
        <h3>Excel & Spreadsheets</h3>
        <p>
          Learn formulas, sorting, filtering, tables and practical
          spreadsheet analysis.
        </p>
      </div>

      <div class="step">
        <span class="step-number">03</span>
        <h3>Data Analysis</h3>
        <p>
          Learn how to clean, explore and interpret data.
        </p>
      </div>

      <div class="step">
        <span class="step-number">04</span>
        <h3>SQL</h3>
        <p>
          Learn how to retrieve and work with data stored in relational
          databases.
        </p>
      </div>

      <div class="step">
        <span class="step-number">05</span>
        <h3>Data Visualization</h3>
        <p>
          Communicate findings through effective charts, dashboards and
          visual stories.
        </p>
      </div>

    </div>
  </div>
</section>
