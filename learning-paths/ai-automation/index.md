      <h2>Learn AI in the right order.</h2>

      <p>
        You don't need to learn everything about AI. Start with the
        fundamentals and progressively move toward practical applications.
      </p>
    </div>

    <div class="steps-grid">

      <div class="step">
        <span class="step-number">01</span>
        <h3>AI Fundamentals</h3>
        <p>
          Understand what AI is, how modern AI systems work at a high
          level, and where they are used.
        </p>
      </div>

      <div class="step">
        <span class="step-number">02</span>
        <h3>Generative AI</h3>
        <p>
          Learn how generative AI can create, transform and analyze
          different types of content.
        </p>
      </div>

      <div class="step">
        <span class="step-number">03</span>
        <h3>Prompting</h3>
        <p>
          Learn how to structure instructions and context to get more
          useful results from AI tools.
        </p>
      </div>

      <div class="step">
        <span class="step-number">04</span>
        <h3>AI for Work</h3>
        <p>
          Apply AI to research, writing, analysis, planning and everyday
          productivity.
        </p>
      </div>

      <div class="step">
        <span class="step-number">05</span>
        <h3>Automation</h3>
        <p>

          work.
        </p>
      </div>

    </div>
