# Affiliate Disclosure

SkillNorth is an independent learning and career discovery website. Some links to course providers may be affiliate links.

When a reader enrolls through an affiliate link, SkillNorth may receive a commission at no additional cost to the reader. Affiliate relationships do not determine which skills, courses or learning paths we recommend.

We aim to keep provider facts separate from our editorial judgments, disclose affiliate relationships near relevant links, and review course information when provider details change.

## Alison

Alison currently states that its affiliate programme pays affiliates 20% of revenue generated by new learners they refer. Its current help documentation says payment can be made by PayPal or bank transfer once the affiliate balance reaches €100. Alison's programme terms state that revenue share is earned on qualifying revenue generated within 12 months of a new unregistered user completing registration.

Always check Alison's current affiliate terms for the latest conditions.
