## Quick answer

The best course depends on your current level, the skill you need, the time you can commit and what you want to do with the skill. SkillNorth uses those factors to turn a large course catalog into a smaller set of learning decisions.

## What to look for

A useful course should have a clear audience, a realistic scope, practical learning outcomes and enough context for a beginner to know what to do next. Avoid choosing by title alone.

### Match the course to the skill

Start with the underlying skill rather than the provider. Explore the [related SkillNorth skill guide]({ '/skills/'+skill+'/' | relative_url }), then use a learning path when you need more than one course.

### Match the level to your starting point

Beginner content should explain concepts and vocabulary. Intermediate content can assume some foundation and spend more time on applied tasks.

### Check the time commitment

A shorter course can be useful for orientation. A longer sequence may be better when the goal is to build a durable skill stack.
